Privacy & data

A plain-language summary of what SHINKAFIT stores and why.

What stays on your device

If you use SHINKAFIT without an account, everything — favorites, workout history, profile and preferences — is stored only in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

What an account adds

Creating an account lets your favorites, history, profile and preferences sync to our database so they follow you to another device. We use Supabase for authentication and storage, with Row Level Security enabled so your data is only ever readable by you and, in aggregate and anonymized form, by SHINKAFIT administrators for basic product statistics.

Account information

  • Email address and password (passwords are never visible to us — they are handled entirely by the authentication provider).
  • Display name and any profile preferences you choose to fill in (experience level, goals, equipment).

Usage analytics

With your consent (on by default, toggle it off any time in Settings), we record anonymous, aggregate product events — such as which workout types are viewed or completed — tied to a random device ID, never your name or email. We do not track your health data, workout performance numbers, or exact location.

What we never collect

  • Payment card details (Premium, if it launches, is handled entirely by a licensed payment provider).
  • Precise location data.
  • Any data from anyone we know to be under the age required by our provider's terms.

Your choices

  • Export all of your data at any time from Settings or the Progress page.
  • Clear the data stored on this device from Settings, without affecting your cloud account.
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data from Settings.

Contact

Questions about your data can be raised through the project's GitHub repository issue tracker.
